Our Mission in Ghana

Built for Ordinary Ghanaians Looking for Real Rooms

"Your house hunt, without the unnecessary walking."

The Real Problem We Are Solving

In Ghana, finding a decent room to rent has traditionally been one of the most frustrating experiences of everyday life. Students, national service personnel, teachers, healthcare workers, and families spend days walking under the hot afternoon sun from one house to another.

Along the way, you pay "agent registration fees" (often GH₵ 100 to GH₵ 300) just for a middleman to show you rooms that are either already occupied, in terrible condition, or completely different from what you were told.

Why RoomHuntah is Different

  • ✓ Exclusively for Room Rentals: We are not a luxury penthouse sales site, land brokerage, or multi-million dollar property investment marketplace. We focus 100% on real rooms and houses for rent.
  • ✓ Video Walkthroughs First: Renters see the exact condition of the floor, ceiling, toilet, and water setup before spending 1 Cedi on transportation.
  • ✓ Transparent Pricing: Always listed in Ghana Cedis (GH₵) with clear advance rent requirements (1 year or 2 years) and security deposit breakdown.
  • ✓ Ghanaian Essentials Priority: We specifically track whether a room has its own separate ECG prepaid meter, poly tank backup, and inside washroom.
"We believe finding a place to lay your head in Ghana should be as simple and dignified as checking your phone."
