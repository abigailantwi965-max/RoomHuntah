Essential Renter Safety

House Hunting Safety Rules in Ghana

Protect your money and avoid rental scams by following these golden rules:

🛑
Rule #1: NEVER send Mobile Money (MoMo) before physical inspection

Scammers often claim "someone else is about to take the room, send GH₵ 500 reservation fee". Never send money until you have physically stood inside the room and verified keys.

⚡
Rule #2: Verify the ECG Prepaid Meter

Ensure the room is truly on its own separate meter (if advertised as self-meter) and check that there is no inherited debt left by a previous tenant.

💧
Rule #3: Turn on the Tap to Test Water Pressure

Turn on the pipe in the bathroom and kitchen. Ask where the poly tank is located and whether water flows regularly from Ghana Water or a borehole.

📑
Rule #4: Sign a Written Tenancy Agreement

Ensure you receive a signed receipt and written tenancy agreement stating the exact advance period (e.g. 1 year) and the landlord's genuine contact information.
